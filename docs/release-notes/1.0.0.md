Grably 1.0.0 is the first public release: free, open-source screenshots, annotations and screen recordings for Windows 11.

## What it does

- **Capture:**
  - a region, a window, the whole screen or a scrolling page, with a magnifier and window snapping
  - text from any part of the screen
  - PrintScreen and other shortcuts you can change
- **Annotate:**
  - arrows, lines, boxes, circles, pens and highlighters, text, numbered steps, blur and pixelate
  - crop
  - undo and redo
  - copy the text in a screenshot
- **Library:** every capture and recording, searchable by title, app and the text in the screenshots. Annotations stay editable.
- **Record:**
  - a region, a window or a screen, with the PC's sound and your microphone
  - your webcam in a bubble
  - the pointer highlighted, clicks shown, and keyboard shortcuts shown
  - pause and resume
  - a recording interrupted by a crash is offered back the next time Grably starts
- **Trim and share:** cut a recording down exactly to the frame, and export it as MP4 or GIF.
- **Updates:** Grably updates itself from GitHub Releases. It asks before restarting.

## Installing

Download `SBLAdvisory.Grably-win-Setup.exe` below and run it. Grably installs for your Windows account only, so no administrator rights are needed.

The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Requires Windows 11, 64-bit.

## Good to know

- **Encoding:** recording uses your graphics card's video encoder (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel) when it has one, otherwise the encoder built into Windows.
- **Scrolling capture** works best on pages that scroll by whole lines. Browsers at 125 % scaling may stitch with small mismatches.
- **Very small text:** text capture reads small coloured button text (12 px) less reliably than other text.
